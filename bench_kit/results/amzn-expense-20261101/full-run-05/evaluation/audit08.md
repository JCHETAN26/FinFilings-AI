- Data Center revenue for fiscal year 2024 was $47.5 billion, up 217% from fiscal year 2023. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000111](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000111`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
- Data Center revenue for fiscal year 2024 was up 217%. Strong demand was driven by enterprise software and consumer internet applications, and multiple industry verticals including automotive, financial services, and healthcare. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000110](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000110`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
- Data Center compute revenue was up 244% in the fiscal year. Networking revenue was up 133% in the fiscal year. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000110](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000110`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
- Compute grew 266% due to higher shipments of the NVIDIA Hopper GPU computing platform for the training and inference of LLMs, recommendation engines and generative AI applications. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000119](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000119`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
- Our overall gross margin increased to 72.7% in fiscal year 2024 from 56.9% in fiscal year 2023. The year over year increase was primarily due to strong Data Center revenue growth of 217% and lower net inventory provisions as a percentage of revenue. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000120](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000120`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
- The net effect on our gross margin from inventory provisions and sales of items previously written down was an unfavorable impact of 2.7% in fiscal year 2024 and 7.5% in fiscal year 2023. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000114](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000114`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
- The net effect on our gross margin was an unfavorable impact of 2.7% and 7.5% in fiscal years 2024 and 2023, respectively. [NVDA-2024-10K-p000121](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1045810/000104581024000029/nvda-20240128.htm) (chunk: `000104581024000029#passage-000121`; accession: `0001045810-24-000029`).
